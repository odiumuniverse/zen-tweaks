Zen Tweaks

Arc-like tweaks for Zen Browser. Turn each one on or off on the add-on's options page.

  • Sidebar over fullscreen video In compact mode, move the pointer to the left edge of a fullscreen video and the sidebar slides out.
  • Only this space's tabs in the address bar “Switch to tab” stops offering tabs from other spaces, so an address opens in the space you are in.
  • Cmd+T like in Arc Enter in the new-tab bar switches to a matching open tab of this space, the bar opens empty, and new tabs go to the top.
  • Ctrl+J and Ctrl+K in the address bar On macOS, Ctrl+J and Ctrl+K move through the address bar suggestions, like the arrow keys.
  • Ctrl+Tab through recent tabs Ctrl+Tab goes back to the tab of this space you used last, not to the next one in the list.
  • Open a window on Cmd+Tab On macOS, switching to Zen with no windows left opens a new one, like clicking its Dock icon.
  • Close and minimize on the right in Picture-in-Picture On macOS, close and minimize move to the right corner of the Picture-in-Picture window, and “Back to tab” moves to the left.
  • Picture-in-Picture like in Arc The site's domain on top, labelled Minimize and Close, 15-second seeking around play/pause and a thin progress bar. Labels and the domain show only when they fit. A playing video moves to Picture-in-Picture when you leave its tab.
  • Little Arc for links from other apps On macOS, a link from another app opens in a small window with the current space's theme; Cmd+O moves it to the space, Esc closes it, Cmd+Opt+N opens an empty one.
  • Glance like Peek in Arc The buttons next to a Glance become large white circles, as in Arc's Peek.

Install

  1. Type about:config in the address bar, accept the warning, and set these two settings:

    xpinstall.signatures.required false
    extensions.experiments.enabled true

    Zen Tweaks changes Zen's own interface, which regular add-ons can't do, so addons.mozilla.org won't sign it. With the first setting off, Zen accepts unsigned add-ons, but it still asks before each install.

  2. Restart Zen.

  3. Install the add-on. If Zen says it prevented this site from installing software, choose Continue to Installation, then Add.

    Install in Zen

Updates arrive on their own. To pick which tweaks are on, click Zen Tweaks in the extensions panel, or open about:addons, then Zen Tweaks, then Options.
